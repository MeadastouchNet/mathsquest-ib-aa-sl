Scope: AA SL current course, through 2028. No AHL-only content. This is a compact course supplement, with one representative worked example and six questions per syllabus subtopic; it does not replace a textbook's full exercise bank. The graph-check method is taught explicitly, including endpoint and tangent-root checks.
