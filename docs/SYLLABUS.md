## Student requirements

Student guide: 150-hour allocation; Paper 1 (90 minutes/80 marks/no technology/40%), Paper 2 (90 minutes/80 marks/GDC/40%), exploration (20 marks/20%). The 30-hour inquiry allocation differs from the roughly 10–15 teaching hours recommended for direct exploration work. Current IA criteria: A4/B4/C3/D3/E6; approximately 12–20 double-spaced pages including diagrams, excluding bibliography. School dates, device approval, exam-mode instructions and access arrangements require local confirmation. No grade boundaries or admission guarantees are invented.
